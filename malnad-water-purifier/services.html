New Purifier Installation

You bought a new purifier and need it fitted correctly.

We fit the purifier, connect it to the water supply and the power point, and check that it works.

  • Check of the fitting position and water connection
  • Fitting of the purifier and the pipes
  • Test run and simple usage advice

Water Purifier Repair

Your purifier does not start, makes noise or the water tastes different.

We check the purifier, find the cause and explain the repair before we start the work.

  • Check of the pump, power supply and valves
  • Diagnosis of the fault
  • Repair or part replacement after your approval

RO System Servicing

Your RO purifier has not been serviced for a long time.

A general service of the RO system to help it work well.

  • Cleaning of the filter housings and the tank
  • Check of the filters and the membrane
  • Check of the water flow and the TDS level

Filter & Membrane Replacement

Your filters or membrane are due for change, or the water quality has changed.

We replace the filters or the RO membrane with parts that suit your purifier model.

  • Check of the present filters
  • Replacement of the filters or the membrane
  • Test of the water after replacement

Water Leakage Troubleshooting

Water leaks from the purifier, the pipes or the tank.

We find the source of the leak and fix it.

  • Check of the pipes, connectors and fittings
  • Check of the filter housings and the tank
  • Replacement of worn parts if necessary

Low Water Flow Troubleshooting

The purifier gives very little water or fills the tank slowly.

We find the cause of the low flow, for example blocked filters, the membrane, the pump or the inlet pressure.

  • Check of the inlet water pressure
  • Check of the filters, the membrane and the pump
  • Repair or replacement after your approval
